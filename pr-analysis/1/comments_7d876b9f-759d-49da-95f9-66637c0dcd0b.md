### [P3] Calibration thresholds are hard-coded against the fixture's test count

`evals/coding-agent/calibrate-audio-inventory.test.js:29` and `evals/coding-agent/calibrate-audio-inventory.test.js:33-34` (origin/main)

The controls assert `'running 7 tests'` (:29), `'5 passed; 2 failed'` (:33) and `'7 passed'` (:34). These are explicit — which is right for an eval — but they silently assume the fixture keeps exactly seven outcomes and that the pre-fix parent fails exactly the two duplicate-inventory ones. If someone adds an eighth fixture outcome (e.g. a default-device or sample-rate outcome), every one of these strings breaks at once and the failure messages won't point at the count coupling. A one-line comment cross-referencing `README.md`'s "Seven outcomes … nine calibration controls" paragraph (also added in `fdf871c46`) or a shared constant for the expected counts would make the coupling maintainable. No behavior bug — the suite fails loudly rather than passing vacuously, which is the important property.
