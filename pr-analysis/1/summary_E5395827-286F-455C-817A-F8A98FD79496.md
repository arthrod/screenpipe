## Risks / quality issues found (details in comments file)

1. **P2 — coverage.md rows at `coverage.md:153` and `:155` cite tests deleted later in
   this same PR.** Commit `1955e12b8` ("Gmail Trigger every 5 s, fast English AI ticket",
   PR-only, 10-18 14:30) removed "reads unread emails of the support inbox…", "every
   activation catches up the unread emails…" and "the AI Agent's tools are ScreenPipe's
   history search…" (`git log -S`), replacing them with opposite-semantics tests
   (`novadesk-support-tickets.test.ts:76` "read or unread", `:159` "has no tools") and
   switching the workflow to `n8n-nodes-base.gmailTrigger` (workflow JSON line 9).
   coverage.md (committed 11:06 the same day, never updated) still says at `coverage.md:9`
   "every listed test passes" and its rows describe the replaced IMAP/unread-dedupe/tools
   design. The features are still tested, but under different names and semantics; the
   matrix's premise is violated at HEAD.
2. **P2 — issues 13/14 systematically quote French UI/notification strings that exist
   nowhere in the repo** (rg over all of crates/infra/packages/apps). Examples: « Échec à
   l'étape… » vs `activity.rs:151` `Failed at step “{step}”: {error}`; « n8n injoignable,
   tes Flows ne tournent pas » vs `health-banner.tsx:16` "n8n is unreachable: your Flows
   are not running"; « Échec du Flow … » vs `failures.rs:279` `Flow “{name}” failed`;
   outcome labels « Échec/Ignoré/À trier à la main » vs `labels.ts:56-69`
   Failed/Ignored/Sort by hand; section « Activité » vs `flow-activity.tsx:53` "Activity".
   Anyone running issue 13/14's live checks or coverage's live item 5 against the built
   app will look for strings that never appear. `CONTEXT.md` defines no French-quoting
   convention, so this is drift, not house style.
3. **P2 — issue 14 `(the first check looks 24 h back)` is contradicted by the code**:
   `health.rs:173-178` bounds the first check at engine start; the module doc
   (`health.rs:22-24`) and a dedicated passing test

4. **P3 — issue 13 omits the `retried` field** from the activity payload shape
